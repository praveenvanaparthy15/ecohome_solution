Renewable Energy Integration Best Practices

Homes with solar panels can reduce electricity costs by consuming solar energy directly rather than exporting excess generation and purchasing electricity later.

The best time to operate flexible appliances is often during periods of strong solar generation.

Typical solar production increases after sunrise, reaches its strongest period around midday, and decreases toward sunset.

Flexible loads that can often be shifted toward solar production include:
- Electric vehicle charging
- Water heating
- Dishwasher operation
- Washing machines
- Pool pumps
- Battery charging

Solar-aware scheduling should consider both expected generation and electricity prices.

If solar generation is forecast to be low because of clouds or weather conditions, flexible loads may be better scheduled for another period with lower electricity prices.

A useful solar optimization strategy is:
1. Forecast solar generation.
2. Estimate household consumption.
3. Identify flexible devices.
4. Match device operation with expected solar availability.
5. Avoid expensive grid consumption when possible.

The objective is to maximize useful on-site solar consumption while maintaining customer requirements.
