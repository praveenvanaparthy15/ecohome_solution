Smart Home Automation Energy-Saving Best Practices

Smart home devices can reduce energy consumption by automatically coordinating appliances with occupancy, electricity prices, and solar generation.

Use smart plugs or appliance schedules to prevent unnecessary standby consumption.

Schedule flexible appliances such as dishwashers and washing machines during lower-cost electricity periods.

When solar generation is high, prioritize flexible loads such as EV charging, water heating, pool pumps, and appliances.

Use occupancy sensors to reduce HVAC, lighting, and other device usage in empty rooms.

Create automation rules that turn devices off when they are not required.

Avoid running several high-power devices simultaneously during electricity price peaks.

A smart energy management strategy should consider:
- Device power consumption
- Device operating duration
- User-required completion time
- Electricity price
- Solar availability
- Customer comfort preferences

Automation should prioritize customer requirements and should not disable safety-critical systems.
