Seasonal Energy Management Best Practices

Energy consumption changes significantly with seasons and weather.

During summer, cooling demand can increase substantially during hot afternoons. Pre-cooling during lower-cost or high-solar periods can reduce peak electricity usage.

During winter, heating demand can increase during mornings and evenings. Scheduling flexible heating-related loads outside expensive periods can reduce costs.

Spring and autumn generally provide opportunities for reduced HVAC consumption because outdoor temperatures are moderate.

Seasonal strategies should consider:
- Outdoor temperature
- Weather forecast
- Historical energy consumption
- Solar generation
- Electricity prices
- Occupancy patterns

For hot weather:
- Use moderate thermostat settings.
- Use fans to improve comfort.
- Run flexible appliances during solar production.
- Avoid unnecessary peak-hour HVAC operation.

For cold weather:
- Use reasonable thermostat settings.
- Improve insulation and reduce heat loss.
- Schedule flexible electrical loads outside peak periods.

Energy recommendations should adapt to current weather rather than relying only on fixed schedules.
