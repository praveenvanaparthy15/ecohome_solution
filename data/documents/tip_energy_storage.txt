Energy Storage Optimization Best Practices

Home batteries can store electricity generated by solar panels and provide energy later when electricity prices are high.

A battery strategy should consider:
- Current state of charge
- Expected solar generation
- Expected household consumption
- Electricity prices
- Battery capacity
- Backup reserve requirements

When electricity prices are low and solar generation is limited, grid charging may sometimes be economical if the battery system and tariff allow it.

When strong solar generation is expected, prioritize charging the battery with excess solar energy after meeting immediate household demand.

During expensive peak periods, stored energy can reduce grid electricity purchases.

Do not repeatedly charge and discharge a battery without considering efficiency losses and battery operating constraints.

Maintain any customer-defined backup reserve before using stored energy for cost optimization.

A good optimization sequence is:
1. Estimate solar generation.
2. Estimate household demand.
3. Check battery state of charge.
4. Compare electricity prices.
5. Determine whether solar charging, grid charging, or discharging is beneficial.
6. Preserve the required backup reserve.
